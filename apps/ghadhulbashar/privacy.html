Privacy Policy (100% On-Device)

Ghadhul Bashar (غض البصر) operates strictly inside your browser with zero external server communication.

Zero Data Collection or Transmission

  • No images, video frames, URLs, or browsing history are ever uploaded or logged remotely.
  • All 4 quantized ONNX AI models run locally on your device via WebGPU or WASM SIMD.
  • Zero analytics, crash telemetry, advertising trackers, or external CDNs exist in the package.

Local Browser Storage

  • chrome.storage.local stores your Fiqh profile, mask style, allowlist, and offline PBKDF2-SHA-256 Time-Lock/PIN hash.
  • IndexedDB (gb_vision_cache_v1) caches perceptual dHash verdicts locally for <0.1 ms lookup and can be cleared anytime in Options.

Safe Configuration Exports

JSON configuration exports automatically strip offline Time-Lock/Partner PIN hashes and salts.
